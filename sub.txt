# Rotation

Shadow Blades
Dance when SecTec ready
Vanish -> Shadowstrike

Shadowstrike for AA optimization on 1-4 targets
Finish on 6 CP:
    SecTec
    Coup
    Black Powder if 3+ targets, 2 targets with Potent Powder
    Eviscerate
Build if Dance or 60 Energy:
    Shadowstrike if 1-3 targets
    Shuriken Storm if 2+ targets
    Backstab

# Dance
Trickster prefers high CP (SecTec), DS prefers low CP (Storm)
Hold Dance if Shadow Blades cooldown < 9
Dance twice in Blades - 1 gcd gap for high/low CP preferences is good
Dance is affected by lag, x.4-x.5 feels necessary

# Notes
Haste trinkets must be pressed BEFORE Dance to get snapshot
Hold CP for Dance+SecTec up to 3 sec
Might be some dry SecTec opportunities with high haste
